# Taste and direction

- pacing-and-structure: Choose a promise, progression and payoff before effects.
- hooks: Open with a concrete question, result or visible transformation.
- typography-and-pairing: Use at most two available font families.
- color-and-palettes: Start with brand colors and one restrained accent.
- motion-principles-and-easing: Animate a change in meaning, position or focus.
- transition-grammar: Carry a subject, direction or shape across each cut.
- caption-styles: Keep captions aligned to trustworthy word timestamps.
- platform-specs: Choose aspect and safe areas before layout.
- audio-and-music-mixing: Let speech lead scene timing.
- talking-head-recipe: Preserve the speaker and original footage as the base.
- product-demo-recipe: Show an actual problem, action and supported result.
- launch-teaser-recipe: Build anticipation, reveal and a clear next action.
