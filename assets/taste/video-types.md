Beat outlines with share of total length. Recipe ids are in assets/recipes/.

- Product launch, 15 s: hook-title 15 percent, product-reveal 35, kinetic-list 30,
  outro-cta 20. Pacing 3, look clean-product or bold-social.
- Social tip, 15 to 30 s: hook-title 15, step-explainer 60, outro-cta 25. Pacing 4.
- Stat story, 15 s: hook-title 20, stat-callout 50, quote 0 or 15, outro-cta 15 to 30. Pacing 3.
- Testimonial, 30 s: hook-title 10, quote 50, talking-head with lower third 25, outro-cta 15.
  Pacing 2, look calm-documentary.
- Explainer, 60 s: hook-title 8, section-card plus step-explainer repeated 3 times 75,
  before-after 0 or 10, outro-cta 7 to 17. Pacing 2.
- Every video ends on a call to action held at least 2.0 s unless the brief has no goal.
- Avoid: more than 5 scenes in 15 s; a section card in videos under 30 s.
