id: captions
title: Captions and reading speed
tags: [captions, text, accessibility]
---
- Words per line: at most 5 for 9:16, 7 for 1:1, 9 for 16:9.
- Lines per caption: at most 2; one line preferred for 9:16.
- Reading speed: at most 3.0 words per second (about 17 characters per second); never shorter
  than 0.8 s on screen.
- Captions match audio timing within 0.1 s: appear at the first word's start, leave at the last
  word's end plus 0.15 s.
- Break lines at phrase boundaries, never between an article and its noun.
- Position: inside the platform's caption safe area (see platform-specs); never over a face.
- Size: the look's caption role (see typography); weight 600 or more; scrim or outline when the
  background varies.
- Highlight the current word with the accent only in social styles; one highlighted word at a time.
- Avoid: all caps for more than 4 words; emoji inside captions unless the brief asks.
