---
id: transitions
title: Transitions between scenes
tags: [transitions, cuts, structure]
---
- Default is a hard cut. Use a designed transition at most once per 3 scenes.
- Transition length: 0.25 to 0.5 s for social, 0.4 to 0.8 s for landscape, 1.0 s maximum.
- Match the motion direction of the scene: if elements rise, the transition wipes up.
- Types by intent: cut (continuity), crossfade (time passing, 0.5 s), push or wipe (next step in a
  list), zoom through (reveal detail), section card (chapter change, 1.2 to 2.0 s hold).
- Cut on action: start the next scene's first entrance within 0.1 s of the cut.
- A whoosh starts 0.1 to 0.15 s before a push or wipe (see sound-design).
- Avoid: mixing more than two transition types in a video; transitions under captions mid-word;
  star wipes, page curls and 3D cube spins.
