title: Pacing and shot length
tags: [pacing, timing, structure]
---
Pacing level maps to average shot length (seconds). The brief slider shows these numbers.

| Level | Name | Avg shot | Min shot | Max shot | Cuts per 15 s |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | calm | 4.5 | 2.5 | 8.0 | 3 to 4 |
| 2 | steady | 3.2 | 1.8 | 6.0 | 4 to 5 |
| 3 | lively | 2.2 | 1.2 | 4.0 | 6 to 7 |
| 4 | punchy | 1.5 | 0.8 | 3.0 | 9 to 10 |
| 5 | rapid | 1.0 | 0.5 | 2.0 | 14 to 15 |

- Vary shot length by 20 to 40 percent around the average; equal lengths feel mechanical.
- The first shot after the hook may be 1.5 times the average to let the viewer settle.
- Hold the final call to action at least 2.0 s regardless of level.
- Text on screen needs its reading time (see captions) even when the level is high; extend the shot,
  do not shrink the text.
- Default level by platform: short vertical social 4, landscape explainer 2, product launch 3,
  documentary 1.
- Avoid: more than three consecutive shots under 1.0 s; any shot under 0.4 s except flash cuts.
