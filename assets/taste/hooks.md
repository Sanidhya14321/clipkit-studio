title: Hooks and openings
tags: [hooks, opening, retention]
---
The hook is the first 0 to 3 s. Its job is to make the viewer stay for the next 3.

- Something moves or changes by 0.3 s; no static first frame.
- The main claim or question is readable by 1.2 s, at most 7 words.
- Hook text is the largest type in the video (display size of the look's type scale).
- Hook styles: bold claim, question, number or stat, before and after flash, pattern interrupt,
  direct address. Pick one; do not stack two.
- A riser or hit lands 0.1 to 0.2 s before the hook text lands (see sound-design).
- Short social: payoff of the hook within 5 s. Landscape explainer: within 10 s.
- Avoid: logos or titles as the first frame, slow fades from black over 0.5 s, "In this video".
