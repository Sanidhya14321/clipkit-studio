title: Sound design placement
tags: [audio, sfx, sound-design]
---
Offsets are relative to the visual event; negative means before it.

| Event | Sound | Offset | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| push or wipe | whoosh | -0.12 s | 0.3 to 0.6 s |
| text landing (display or title) | soft hit | -0.02 s | 0.1 to 0.3 s |
| hook | riser | ends at -0.05 s | 0.8 to 2.0 s |
| stat counter end | tick or pop | 0 s | 0.1 s |
| scene cut in dialogue | room tone | spans the cut | cut plus 0.2 s |

- At most one sound effect per 1.5 s; skip a hit rather than stack two.
- Do not place effects under spoken words that carry the main claim.
- Calm looks use half as many effects as bold looks.
- Avoid: cartoon sounds unless the brief tone is playful; the same sample more than 3 times in 15 s.
