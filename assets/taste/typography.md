Sizes are a fraction of frame height so they work at any resolution.

| Role | 9:16 | 16:9 | 1:1 | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| display | 0.075 | 0.110 | 0.090 | 800 |
| title | 0.055 | 0.080 | 0.065 | 700 |
| body | 0.035 | 0.050 | 0.042 | 500 |
| caption | 0.032 | 0.045 | 0.038 | 600 |
| label | 0.024 | 0.032 | 0.028 | 600 |

- Minimum on-screen size is the label size; nothing smaller.
- At most two font families per video: one display, one text. A look may use one for both.
- Line height 1.05 to 1.15 for display, 1.3 for body. Tracking -0.02 em for display, 0 for body.
- At most 3 lines for a title, 2 for a caption, about 32 characters per line for 9:16.
- Contrast of text against what is behind it: at least 4.5 to 1; 3 to 1 only for display above
  0.08 of frame height.
- Emphasis by weight or color from the look, never by underline or a third family.
