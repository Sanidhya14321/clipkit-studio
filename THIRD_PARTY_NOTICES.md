# Third-party notices: 0.3.0

The rejected 0.2 stock/template packs are no longer bundled. Their code, source notices and resource licenses remain in a separately distributed checksum-pinned compatibility archive for existing projects. Historical Git releases retain their original inventories.

- **Video Forge 0.7.1 original work — MIT:** selected creative-direction/rhythm guidance, twelve shot recipes, frame math, split type, perspective planes and wipes. Original LICENSE and NOTICE ship in packages/motion. `sources.json` records original/adapted checksums and modifications. Standalone rendering/provider workflows and private product captures are excluded.
- **Paper Design Shaders 0.0.82 — Apache-2.0:** the frame-driven material adapter calls the installed runtime. Its unmodified LICENSE and NOTICE ship in packages/motion/vendor-notices and in the dependency package. The adapter stops autonomous playback and seeks explicit frame time.
- **Inter and Space Grotesk — SIL OFL 1.1:** existing pinned font files and original notices remain in public/vendor/manifest.json. They support the editor and scene typography, not a stock-media catalog.
- **Lucide — ISC plus inherited MIT where applicable:** installed editor icons retain package notices and the pinned public/vendor notice files.
- **React, esbuild, YAML and other npm infrastructure:** their installed package notices remain applicable. Remotion uses its separate framework license; adopted-source MIT/Apache permissions do not replace it.

Motion and selected Codrops projects are mechanism references listed in packages/motion/references.json. No sample media, docs corpus or GSAP runtime is copied from those references. Do not describe reference-only repositories as bundled assets or certify generated work as human-made.

The demonstration source was authored through real Codex scene operations and subsequently refined and edited. Its rhythmic sound is newly synthesized. Its editor evidence consists of actual localhost recordings, cropped for readability. Captures and original example source are MIT under this repository's license; the fonts and runtime notices remain separate. User-imported media retains user-supplied provenance and permissions.

Every export writes a credits JSON sidecar. Demo project delivery excludes provider keys, server tokens and agent-workspace configuration.
